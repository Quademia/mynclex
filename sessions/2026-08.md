### ⭐ THE ONE REAL BUG — and Sam found it, not me

Sam, in the student workspace: *"I still see the other programs… i think
its probably an rls issue, becuase is the same id viwing as tutor and
stufent."* He was exactly right.

Postgres **ORs** the SELECT policies on a table. `nclex_programmes`
carries two — `nclex_programmes_student_select`
(has-enrolment) and `nclex_programmes_self_select` (`tutor_id =
auth.uid()`). `getMyAccessibleProgrammesAction` deliberately had **no
enrolment filter in its query body**; its own header comment said RLS
"narrows to enrolled-only automatically". True for a student-only
account, where the ownership branch can never fire. False for anyone who
is both, who then sees every programme they authored in the STUDENT
list.

**Which is why clearing his enrolments could never have fixed it** — and
I had twice tried to fix the symptom before he named the cause. The
lesson worth keeping: **RLS is a security boundary, not a view filter.**
A surface that means "things I am enrolled in" has to say so itself.
Fixed in `lib/programmes/student-actions.ts`, two lines. Listing only —
`require-programme-access` always gated entry, so it was never an access
hole. vitest 855/855; tsc at the two known pre-existing errors.

### Three traps that cost real time

**1. `nclex_programmes_seed_units_trg` fights deterministic ids.** It
fires on every programme INSERT and creates `length_units` blank units
with random ids, which collide on `(programme_id, unit_index)`. Both
programme seeds drop the trigger's rows before inserting their own.
Bumping `length_units` later does not dodge it — a reconcile trigger
fires on that too.

**2. Attendance does not follow the enrolment.**
`nclex_cohort_session_attendance` is keyed `(session_id, student_id)`
with no enrolment in the key, so deleting enrolments strands the
register rows on a cohort the student has left. **Bit me twice** — once
on Claudia (a June mark against the old Bootcamp still counting toward
"sessions attended"), once on Steven. Delete attendance FIRST.

**3. Hand-carried data needs a read-back assertion, not care.** The
CLOZE and HIGHLIGHT answer shapes fell through my single-answer branch
and wrote 18 NULLs; Steven's two "87%" sittings were actually 100%
because a flat correctness roll passed every item on two 5-item quizzes.
Both found by counting rows afterwards, neither by inspection.

  `nclex_refresh_item_response_stats()` returns 0 and the
  answer-distribution strip stays dark despite 337 submitted answers.
  Deliberate flag; Sam's call to flip.
- `embed_max_blocks_per_note` raised 2 → 5 (the code's own default) so
  the Learn-by-Doing product is not thin.
- Quiz activities seeded in SQL bypass the activity-save auto-mirror, so
  `nclex_programme_quizzes` needs writing by hand. The flagship had 0
  rows against 7 quizzes until this session.
- Only MCQ / SATA / TF / SELECT_N are embeddable in a note. NGN types
  route to quizzes — the embed answer model has no partial credit.
- Tutor quizzes reach students **only** through a programme or cohort.
  There are exactly two student routes. But a programme is a thin
  wrapper, which is the whole premise of `08-maternity.sql`.
- The repo has **no prettier config**, so `npx prettier` reformats to
  double quotes and churns the file. Do not run it.
