**⭐ FOUR TIMES TODAY THE DOCUMENTED CAUSE WAS THE WRONG ONE.** That is the
theme worth carrying forward, not any individual fix:

1. **The runner-mobile doc blamed `.rn-split`'s 924px floor** for three
   sessions. The real floor is `.rn-cjmm-strip`, a nowrap flex row

2. **`use-is-compact.ts` and the mobile stylesheet both stated** that the
   container query and the media query "always agree because in production

   the case panel while the JS never rendered the summary card replacing

3. **`runner.css` calls `.rn-q-wrap`'s `margin: 0 auto` "a no-op inside a
