# CAT test-pool topic checklist (2026 NCLEX-RN Test Plan)

Anti-clustering guide for the generation workflow. Distilled from the activity
statements in the *2026 NCLEX-RN® Test Plan* (effective April 2026). Each
authoring agent is assigned a subcategory + difficulty band and should spread
its items across that subcategory's content areas below — not cluster on one
(e.g. don't make every Physiological Adaptation item cardiac).

**Blueprint weights (2026 midpoints)** — used to size how many items each
subcategory gets per band (600/band):

| Subcategory | Weight | Per band (of 600) | ×3 bands |
|---|---|---|---|
| Management of Care | 18% | 108 | 324 |
| Safety and Infection Control* | 13% | 78 | 234 |
| Health Promotion and Maintenance | 9% | 54 | 162 |
| Psychosocial Integrity | 9% | 54 | 162 |
| Basic Care and Comfort | 9% | 54 | 162 |
| Pharmacological and Parenteral Therapies | 16% | 96 | 288 |
| Reduction of Risk Potential | 12% | 72 | 216 |
| Physiological Adaptation | 14% | 84 | 252 |

\* The 2026 plan renamed this to "Safety and Infection Prevention and Control";
we use the current codebase string "Safety and Infection Control" for
consistency (see cat.html §19).

## Safe and Effective Care Environment

### Management of Care
Admit/transfer/discharge · handoff (SBAR) · delegation & supervision (LPN/UAP
scope) · client advocacy & rights · prioritisation by acuity · quality/performance
improvement · multidisciplinary collaboration · verifying & implementing orders ·
advance directives · unbiased/equitable care · informed consent · referrals ·
plan-of-care initiation & update · confidentiality/privacy (HIPAA, social media) ·
mandatory reporting (abuse, communicable disease) · conflict management · ethics &
ethical dilemmas · legal scope of practice · documentation terminology · time
management · cost-effective care · case management.

### Safety and Infection Control
Infection prevention (hand hygiene, aseptic/sterile technique, isolation,
standard/transmission-based precautions, PPE) · injury prevention · treatment-order
verification · biohazard/hazardous material handling · security (newborn, violence,
controlled access) · ergonomics/safe handling · error & near-miss reporting · client
identification · safe equipment use · emergency preparedness/response · restraints ·
allergy assessment · reporting unsafe staff practice · home/environmental safety
assessment.

## Health Promotion and Maintenance
Prenatal & postpartum care/education · antepartum & labor · newborn/infant/toddler
(birth–2y) · preschool/school-age/adolescent (3–17y) · adult (18–64y) · older adult
(65y+) · growth & development milestones · targeted screening (vision, nutrition,
depression) · high-risk behavior education (smoking, substance) · preventive care &
immunizations · comprehensive health assessment · readiness/barriers to learning ·
home-care ability assessment · family/population/community health risks ·
communication-barrier resources · lifestyle & health maintenance.

## Psychosocial Integrity
Psychosocial factor assessment · abuse/neglect screening & reporting · substance
misuse/withdrawal/toxicities · acute & chronic mental illness (depression, anxiety,
bipolar, schizophrenia, dementia, eating disorders, addictions) · therapeutic
environment/milieu · cultural & spiritual care · end-of-life care · violence
potential & safety precautions · support systems · coping with life changes ·
therapeutic communication · behavioral management (de-escalation) · nonverbal cues ·
sensory/cognitive alterations · grief & loss.

## Physiological Integrity

### Basic Care and Comfort
ADLs & assistance · enteral/tube feedings · postmortem care · irrigations (bladder,
ear, eye) · assistive devices & positioning · skin/tissue integrity & pressure-injury
prevention · bowel/bladder elimination · orthopedic devices (cast, traction) ·
dignity & privacy · nonpharmacologic comfort measures · nutrition status &
intervention · sleep/rest · intake & output · mobility & ROM · pain assessment &
nonpharmacologic management · complementary therapies.

### Pharmacological and Parenteral Therapies
Medication-order appropriateness/accuracy · rights of administration · dosage
calculation · IV infusion monitoring & site care · controlled substances · pre-admin
data review (contraindications, labs, allergies, interactions) · titration to
parameters · blood-product administration & reactions · central venous access
devices · medication education · response evaluation · parenteral (TPN) nutrition ·
pain-management medications · medication reconciliation · high-risk medications ·
safe storage & disposal. Common classes: anticoagulants, insulin, digoxin, opioids,
antibiotics, diuretics, antihypertensives, electrolytes, chemo, psychotropics.

### Reduction of Risk Potential
Vital-sign changes & trends · focused assessments · trend recognition & intervention
· diagnostic-test monitoring · in-scope testing (ECG, POC, fetal monitoring) ·
response to procedures/treatments · pre/post-operative education & care · moderate
sedation · blood/specimen collection · injury/complication prevention · procedure
education · NG/GI tube · urinary catheter · peripheral IV · venous-return devices
(SCDs, antiembolism) · percutaneous feeding tube · lab-value interpretation.

### Physiological Adaptation
Drainage devices (surgical drains, chest tubes, NPWT) · emergency care procedures ·
mechanical ventilation · wound care & dressing changes · invasive-procedure assist
(central line, thoracentesis, bronchoscopy) · ostomy care · postoperative care ·
peritoneal dialysis / hemodialysis / CRRT · suctioning · pulmonary hygiene ·
telemetry · impaired ventilation/oxygenation · temperature regulation · phototherapy
· pacing devices · arterial lines · fluid & electrolyte imbalance · hemodynamics/
perfusion/hemostasis · pathophysiology of acute & chronic conditions · complication
recognition · internal monitoring (ICP, IUPC). Systems: cardiac, respiratory, renal,
neuro, endocrine, GI, hematologic, multisystem (sepsis, shock).
